Our Story

Where Every Memory Finds Its Perfect Home

Founded in 2008 in the heart of Dahej & Bharuch, Rajesh Framing began with a simple belief: a photograph or achievement is more than paper—it is an emotional memory that deserves to be framed with reverence and permanence.

Over the last fifteen years, we have grown into Gujarat's premier custom framing and digital printing destination. From crystal-beveled float glass frames and modern moisture-proof polymers to personalized drinkware, sublimation mugs, and embossed corporate presentation folders, we blend handcrafted mastery with high-precision digital printing technology.

Our Philosophy

The Four Pillars of Our Excellence

What distinguishes Rajesh Framing is our uncompromising devotion to craftsmanship, technology, and customer relationships.

Quality-Focused Approach

We never cut corners. From utilizing 100% optically pure float glass and acid-free museum mats to reinforced internal miter joints, every piece is built to endure for generations.

Complete Customization

Your vision dictates the outcome. We customize frame depths, mat margins, glass types, standalone desktop standoffs, and personalize mugs and bottles with custom names and art.

Professional Printing

Using 12-color archival pigment inks, 1440 DPI wide-gamut printers, 360° rotary UV machines, and metallic foil debossing, we reproduce every gradient and line with absolute fidelity.

Customer Satisfaction

We stand behind every order. From complimentary digital previews on WhatsApp to multi-layer protective packaging, our clients enjoy peace of mind from inquiry to delivery.
